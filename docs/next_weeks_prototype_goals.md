Next week's prototype goal:

A Teams-based Procurement & Contract Knowledge Copilot

Capabilities:

1. Search approved procurement/legal documents.
2. Answer questions with citations.
3. Identify when knowledge is missing.
4. Capture feedback.
5. Escalate unanswered questions.
6. Produce a simple dashboard.

By next week, the prototype should enable:

Step 1

Open Teams.

Step 2

Talk to your agent.

Example:

What approvals are required before signing a supplier agreement?

Step 3

Receive:

- A grounded answer
- Citations
- Source document references
- Confidence indication
- A feedback option
Step 4

Click:

Notify Content Owner

if the answer is inadequate.
